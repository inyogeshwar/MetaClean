Features\
Remove C2PA credentials\
We strip C2PA and JUMBF credentials added by cameras, Adobe Photoshop, and generative tools. These records are one of the primary signals Instagram checks.\
\
Delete Full EXIF, IPTC & XMP Info\
We delete all EXIF, IPTC and XMP data from your image. GPS coordinates, camera serial numbers, timestamps and device details go out together with the AI markers, so your private details are not exposed on Instagram.\
\
Clean without changing pixels\
We strip metadata directly from file headers. We never recompress, resize, or alter image pixels, so your photo looks exactly the same.\
\
Help prevent false AI labels\
Real photos edited in Lightroom or Photoshop can pick up AI flags from simple retouching. Removing those tags helps prevent Instagram from adding an automated AI label.\
\
Clean up to 5 photos at once\
Drop up to 5 images together, 100 MB in total, and clean them in one click.\
\
Inspect every tag before cleaning\
Review every EXIF, IPTC, and XMP tag found in your file before you clean it, then confirm the records are gone after cleaning.\
\
Works with common image formats\
Clean JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, and HEIC images before uploading them to your Instagram feed, story, or carousel.\
\
Files deleted after 30 minutes\
Uploaded images are processed in temporary memory and permanently deleted within 30 minutes. We never store your files or use them for training.\
\
Free with no account required\
